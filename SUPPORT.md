# Moodary Support

If you need help with Moodary, please use one of the following options:

## Frequently Asked Questions
- How do I track my mood?  
Open the app and tap the mood icon to add a new entry.  

- How do I delete my data?  
Go to Settings > Delete All Data.  

- How do I cancel my Premium subscription?  
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Open iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
